What we found / 1 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

A slow-phone test needs attention.

One mobile test scored 59/100. The main content took 10.1 seconds to appear on a simulated slow connection.

That points to a risk for guests on slower phones. But real-user mobile data passes: its main-content time was 2.3 seconds.

October 30 test; one simulated slow-4G phone, not every guest’s wait. Real-user result covers the latest 28 days.

What we found / 2 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

02

Sacramento’s closing times disagree.

The website says 8:30 p.m. every day. Google says 8 p.m. every day for the same 2700 Capitol Ave location.

A guest could plan a visit using the wrong closing time. Confirm the right hours, then make both places match.

Public website and Google listing checked October 30. This shows a mismatch, not which source is correct.

What we found / 3 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

03

Rocklin is missing from the contact form.

The location page lists five stores. The contact form offers four location choices, with no Rocklin option.

A Rocklin guest cannot choose their store. Add Rocklin and make sure each message reaches the right team.

The location choice is optional; the form is not proven broken. We did not submit a test message.

How we would help / 1 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

01

Make the first screen lighter.

Resize large images, delay work that is not needed right away and reserve space for pictures. Retest on a slow phone. Check real visitors’ results too, not just the score.

02

Keep one trusted set of store details.

Confirm hours with each location. Update the site and Google together, including holiday changes. Give one person a short monthly check to keep them in step.

03
